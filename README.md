# 🏝️ Treasure Island

Welcome to **Treasure Island** — a thrilling multiplayer treasure hunt game inspired by Battleship, with an adventurous island twist! Hide your treasure chests, dig through enemy islands, and outwit your opponent to claim victory! ⚔️💰

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

Imagine you're stranded on a mysterious island. Your mission?

- Hide **5 treasure chests** somewhere on your island.
- Sail to your opponent’s island and start **digging** — one spot at a time.
- The first player to discover **all 5 hidden chests** on the enemy island **wins** the game!

This isn’t just Battleship. It’s **Battleship with palm trees, golden loot, and a pirate’s pride. 🏴‍☠️**

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Modes of Play

You can play Treasure Island in 3 exciting modes:

1. **Play with Bot** 🤖 – Test your skills against our cunning AI.
2. **Quick Match** 🌍 – Get matched with a random online player.
3. **Private Game** 🪙 – Create a game and share the Link with a friend for a 1v1 showdown!

---

## 🚀 Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React.js
- **Backend**: Express.js
- **Real-time Communication**: Socket.IO

---

## 🧰 Features

- 🗺️ Interactive island grid where you can hide your chests
- 💬 Real-time 2-player gameplay using WebSockets
- 🎭 Dynamic bot logic for solo play
- 🔐 Create or join private games with Links
- 🔄 Turn-based digging system
- 🎨 Island themed visuals
